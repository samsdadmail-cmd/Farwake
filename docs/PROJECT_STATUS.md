# FARWAKE — Project Status

FARWAKE is an original, iPhone-first colony simulation in active development.

## Playable builds

- Legacy prototype: https://farwake-beta.vercel.app
- Founders Alpha 0.3: https://farwake-founders.vercel.app

## Design direction

- Deliberately established expedition of five specialists on an unfamiliar planet
- Atmospheric top-down pixel art and touch-first design
- Detailed 5–40-person living colony with autonomous needs, jobs, and relationships
- Blueprint construction and eventual custom building design
- Environmental survival, exploration, diplomacy, and hybrid tactical intervention
- Migration, families, generations, and independently configurable aging
- Seed-generated geography with handcrafted landmarks
- Optional transition to civilization-scale management in a future major expansion

## Principles

Build and test playable slices, preserve legacy builds, and keep save formats versioned.
