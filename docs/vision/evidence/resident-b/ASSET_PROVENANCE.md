# Aya Mercer asset provenance — Checkpoint B

Pilot assets for one established founder, not approval of a broader character style.

The transparent atlases were generated with image generation on 13 November 2026,
then integrated as discrete source frames. No geometric person renderer or white
background is used in the immersive room. JSON sidecars record original dimensions
and alpha bounds. Runtime nearest-neighbour cutouts are cached at gameplay scale;
source artwork is retained unchanged for recoverability.

Interior direction: short charcoal wavy hair, warm peach skin, ochre frontier work
jacket, dark slate trousers and brown boots; practical adult proportions; standing,
short walking, seated reading/page variation and side-lying sleeping/breath variation.
The interior atlas was the identity reference for the smaller directional exterior
atlas. Exterior simplification preserves hair, skin, coat, trousers and boots; its
more compact head/body proportions still require Kyle and Atlas's visual judgment.

Identity is checked against the original Aya profile before using either atlas.
Renamed, recoloured or otherwise different saved characters retain their existing
presentation. Exterior specialised work/leisure rigs are retained in this pilot;
the coordinated atlas replaces Aya's walking and idle views only.

Interior frame order (four columns, two rows): standing; right step A; right step B;
seated/open book; seated/page; sleeping; sleeping/breath; alternate standing.
Exterior frame order (four columns, three rows): down/up/left/right idle; down A/B
and up A/B; left A/B and right A/B.

Furniture masks restore existing lit background pixels in front of the body. They
do not redraw the furniture or alter the approved A2 plates. In the current room
Aya uses the left armchair and lower bunk. No furniture ownership or additional
persistent resident state has been added.

Original interior artwork is also preserved as “Aya Mercer pixel-art frontier
sprite atlas.png”, Library file libfile_d89a00c27f948191ad88a0c03bf33b15. That atlas
is source artwork, not gameplay evidence. Actual evidence is captured in-engine.
