Work in progress; no integrated character creative approval yet. A2's complete-home
composition and optional 1.7× camera are approved foundations and remain intact.

Prototype reads Aya's exact established identity and actual physical attendance.
It draws nobody else. A standing/short approach stage follows actual home settling,
reading follows the active life_home state, and the bunk pose requires actual rest
with action >1 (the existing recovery threshold). No simulation outcomes, memories,
relationships, tasks, housing assignments or save fields are changed by drawing.
The small arrival/bedside transitions follow actual task progress, not wall time.

The authored atlas has standing/short walking, seated reading/page and sleeping
frames. Matching directional Aya miniatures are used for exterior walking/idle;
existing specialised work/leisure rigs remain as before. Other residents are
unchanged. Exact identity guard prevents a custom save's recoloured/renamed person
from being silently painted as this Aya. Missing/not-yet-loaded artwork falls back
to existing exterior presentation and never to an interior geometric person.

Seated raised knees and side-on sleeping art require in-engine review against the
actual left armchair and lower bunk. Foreground arms/table/bedding/ladder reuse
original background pixels as occlusion, rather than painting over furniture.
No claim of good visual fit is made before examining captured gameplay.

are required before expanding this pilot. Review scenes build from starting
resources, save/reload, then allow real autonomous routines/travel; they do not
inject occupants/tasks or grant materials. Exterior and interior captures come
from that same saved colony. Controlled simulation stepping is used only to reach
the evening; the recorded home activity transition runs at ordinary simulation
speed, with clearly visible pause/resume for still captures.
