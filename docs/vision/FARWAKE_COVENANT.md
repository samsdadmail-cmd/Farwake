# FARWAKE Covenant

**A small home in an impossibly big universe.**

FARWAKE is an atmospheric top-down science-fiction colony simulator about people
creating lives together somewhere unfamiliar. The invisible director creates the
conditions in which their stories can happen. Management supports those lives;
it remains deep, consequential and satisfying.

## Home

People have routines, preferences, friendships, memories and histories. They
occupy actual homes and eventually use authored furniture. A home should reveal
something about its inhabitants through genuine events and use, not fabricated
visitors, relationships or decorative activity.

## The Unknown

The alien world is vast, beautiful, mysterious and sometimes dangerous.
Discoveries should eventually have domestic consequences: objects, interests,
absence and reunion. Exploration must grow beyond resource rewards. This is a
future design principle, not authority to expand exploration now.

## Legacy

Day 200 should differ from Day 20 because of what people have lived through.
People and places acquire meaning. History should eventually become visible,
without reducing every memory to a temporary numerical bonus. Preserve player
agency, logistics, optimisation, production and technological achievement.

## Lamplight

A window shines because real residents and available infrastructure justify it.
The player builds and supplies the settlement; residents determine their lives;
the world displays the consequence. Awake occupancy, sleeping occupancy, empty
homes and unavailable power must be distinguishable. Do not add a nightly manual
window-management chore. No fabricated smoke, work, companionship or memories.

## Binding boundaries

Preserve the top-down management overworld. A cinematic interior view is not a
silent conversion of the game camera. Retain v4 saves, supported verified v3
migration, original recovery copies and foreign-writer protection. Never overwrite
original player data to test presentation. No main merge or production promotion
without Kyle's explicit approval.

Kyle is final creative authority; Atlas is design partner/reviewer/coordinator;
Work is the engineering and implementation partner. Independent judgment is
welcome, silent substitution of the vision is not.

Keep the windows lit.
