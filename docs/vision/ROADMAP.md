Status on 13 November 2026: Experience Overhaul remains in development. No creative

## A — One playable home: composition review

One completed Prefab Home, existing v4 state and continued simulation. Preserve
enter/exit/camera restoration; make management independently accessible. Show the
complete cutaway with roof, furniture, foundation and meaningful alien context.
Remove repeated floors, fix day/night, simplify controls, cap mobile surfaces.

Stop here before expanding the visual direction.

## B — One resident, a few essential poses

After A approval: establish authored coherent pixel art for one founder, with
standing, sitting/reading and sleeping at actual furniture anchors. Proper layering,
identity, light and state. Demonstrate real occupancy and obtain visual review.
Do not roll out twenty activities or all households first.

## C — Interaction in motion

After B approval: record exterior → entry → genuine resident activity → concise
resident inspection → exit. Verify transitions, reduced motion and exact restored
camera. Expand only accepted activities and presentation.

## D — Real-save integration

Exercise an established organically developed colony. Target construction,
production, research, freight, power, homes, exploration, social rewards, saves and
v3 recovery. Measure 5/20/40 residents, memory and sustained sessions. Broad legacy
and Chromium/WebKit regression gate, then physical iPhone review. No claims of
physical smoothness from desktop automation.

## E — Overworld visual foundation

Once home direction is established: retain the top-down camera; improve one small
inhabited district's building readability, terrain/vegetation, distinct daylight
and night, truthful lamplight, people and minimal HUD obstruction. Early screenshot
and interaction checkpoint before spreading the art language to every biome/type.

## F — Complete experience integration

Resume the full approved overhaul around the accepted home/overworld language:
activity animation, genuine social encounters, relationships/memories, contextual
research/construction/inspection, environment/audio, accessibility, save protection
and performance. Future Home/Unknown/Legacy systems remain design research until
these foundations pass review.
